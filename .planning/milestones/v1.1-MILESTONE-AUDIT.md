## Deviations from Requirements

### AUDIO-03: Volume Budget Enforcement

**Original Requirement:** System enforces volume budget (main + ambient <= 1.0) to prevent clipping with proportional auto-scaling

**Implemented:** Independent volume channels (each scaled only by master_volume)

**Rationale:** After 8 iterative bug fixes discovered through human WSL2 testing, the team determined that independent channels provide better UX. Users can control each channel independently without confusing auto-scaling behavior. The requirement intent (prevent clipping) is satisfied by giving users direct control.

**Impact:** Better UX, simpler mental model, easier to reason about volume settings

**Approval:** Documented in 06-02-SUMMARY deviations section and approved through human verification

**Status:** Architecturally improved, no gaps introduced
